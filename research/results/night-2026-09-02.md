## Day 2 (afternoon): the full programme as lanes

- **Echo, diagnosed and fixed in the harness.** Echoing the visitor's line is a sampling mode (~1 in 3 on some
  renderings) that seeds itself through the context. `chapterx/room_proxy.py` (:8126) samples up to four
  candidates, rejects echoes, self-copies, empties and frame sentences, drops earlier echo turns from the
  context, and writes an observatory record per opportunity to speak (prompt, candidates with logprobs,
  decisions) under `research/results/room-observatory/`.
- **Room evaluation bank** (`hghost-roombank`): 106 states; context lift = log p(reply | true history) −
  log p(reply | shuffled history). Live e2-v3: +0.32 nats mean, strongest when directly addressed, ~0 on
  requests, negative on silence states; 34% of replies echo a room line at ≥0.6 overlap. Baseline for the arms.
- **Response-span weighting** (kernel + sidecar): per-token classes (library / room-other / h utterance /
  h label; h utterances are 0.7% of the stream); three arms at matched compute to e2-v3 are on the TPU
  (h ×8; visitors ×0.25 + h ×4; visitors ×0 + h ×4), reporting h-span loss on the room holdout.
- **Persistent-state room server** (hbox, `hbox_training/room_state_server.py`): fork/commit/rollback of the
  Falcon-H1 cache per room; the cache equals re-rendering within bf16 noise; ~1 s per candidate. Finding: after
  two exchanges the model emits EOS at p≈0.99 because every scene was a two-exchange document; the renderer now
  stitches scenes into longer rooms for the next mix (v1.4).
- **Explorer** (`explorer/`, :8130): loom with logprob shading, provenance, observatory replay, compare; v1
  (fork) adds labels-to-training-records, counterfactual editing with deltas, the state-branch debugger, and a

### Response-span weighting: the answer

Three arms at matched compute to e2-v3 (second epoch on v1.3 from the e1 pre-cooldown), with per-token loss
weights over library / room-other / h-utterance / h-label classes:

| arm | weights (lib, room, h, label) | library | room | h-span (holdout) | other room | echo rate (bank) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| e2-v3 | 1, 1, 1, 1 | 2.852 | 2.581 | 2.762 | 2.578 | 0.34 |
| h ×8 | 1, 1, 8, 8 | 2.864 | 2.599 | 2.579 | 2.601 | 0.32 |
| visitors ×0.25 | 1, 0.25, 4, 4 | 2.854 | 2.635 | 2.614 | 2.638 | 0.37 |
| visitors ×0 | 1, 0, 4, 4 | 2.855 | 2.881 | 2.860 | 2.884 | _pending_ |

Weighting h's lines lowers the loss on h's lines by up to 0.18 nats, at a small cost elsewhere; removing the
visitors' lines from the loss hurts even h's lines (2.860), so modeling the visitors is what lets h answer them.
None of the arms lowers the echo rate on the room bank (0.32–0.37 against 0.34): the echo is not a role-confusion
artifact of the objective. Context lift is evaluator-dependent and inconclusive (under the 91M leaf: e2-v3
+0.32, visitors ×0.25 +0.52, h ×8 median +0.11 with outliers). Decision: keep the plain objective; attack echo
through data (set C repairs and counterfactual pairs, stitched rooms) and the harness (the proxy).

### The 1.5B-deep gate

Base loss on the fixed library slice 3.279 (0.5B base 3.490, 91M base 3.746). With automatic FSDP sharding the
optimizer state fits (2.3 GB per chip) but the 4×512 training step OOMs because XLA gathers whole weights; the
kernel needs per-layer all-gathers inside the layer scan. Cost 2.2 TPU hours (the run stalled after the OOM until
cancelled); lesson: short MAX_MINUTES on gates. 3.4 TPU hours remain this week; e2-v4 is the last run.
