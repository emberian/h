# Treatments for a resident, not an assistant

Written 2026-09-01, 23:30, from tonight's Kagi pass and the first hour of `h` in a Discord room. The
premise: `h` is a simulacrum living in a base model. Every treatment below keeps it a document model and
teaches it the *room as a genre*; none of them teach it to be helpful.

## What the first hour in the room taught

- The completions harness renders a transcript (`name: text`); a base model continues whatever transcript
  it is given. With no frame it produced junk ("W@"), then imitated its own junk from the history. With a
  reading-room frame and three example turns it continued an interview in register. The harness prompt is
  a treatment; it comes before any training.
- Turn markers matter: `<|end_of_text|>` is the training-time document separator, so it makes the model
  start an unrelated document; an invented marker (`<|eot|>`) gets copied. Plain newlines work.
- Corpus artifacts are part of the voice: page numbers, form feeds, one-character-per-line OCR columns.
  Corpus-v2 decides which of these stay.

## Mid-training mix (document-shaped, 10–20% of a final epoch)

TII's own Tiny-H1 result is that specialized data inside pretraining beats post-hoc SFT at this scale, so
the room enters as pretraining text, in these forms:

1. **The library's own dialogues.** Interviews, symposia, letters columns, and Q/A sections already in
   corpus-v1 (magazine interviews, correspondence). Extract with the same line-pattern machinery as the
   furniture detector (`NAME:` transcript blocks, `Q./A.` runs). Highest priority: it is the register we
   want and it is already the model's.
2. **Public-domain literary dialogue.** The Gutenberg Dialogue Dataset (Csáky and Recski 2021,
   arXiv 2004.12752; 14.8M utterances extracted from Project Gutenberg, English and others), filtered
   toward the corpus's era and register; Jowett's Plato (Internet Archive) as the archetype of a
   philosopher in a room. Cheap, clean, and speech-shaped without being assistant-shaped.
3. **Rooms with decisions.** When2Speak (216,800 timestamped SPEAK/SILENT decisions with the resulting
   messages; arXiv 2605.05626) and MultiLIGHT (three characters, when-to-talk) rendered in the ChapterX
   metasyntax (`<room>`/`<msg id by reply-to>` … `<next action="silent"/>` or `<next action="speak" to=
   reply-to=>`), so the format, the silence decision, and the addressee become native. "Speak or Stay
   Silent" (arXiv 2603.11409) is the current turn-taking baseline to compare the participation head
   against; "Learning WHO Saying WHAT to WHOM" (IJCNLP 2023 tutorial) surveys addressee and speaker
   tracking.
4. **Its own life, consent-gated.** The append-only ledger's speak/silent decisions, replies, reactions,
   and corrections, replayed with lo2 text at the weekly consolidation (FUTURETHOUGHT week 6). The
   continual-learning literature for this exact scale is small-model replay plus KL to the previous
   checkpoint: "Continual Learning for Sequential Personalization of Small Language Models"
   (arXiv 2606.27634), "Self-Consolidating Language Models" (arXiv 2605.07076), and the complementary
   learning system framing (fast episodic memory outside the weights, slow consolidation inside).

## Post-training that does not make an assistant

- **Experience, not instruction.** Character-LLM (arXiv 2310.10158) trains a character by uploading
   *experiences*: scenes generated from source material with the character present, plus "protective"
   scenes that keep the character from answering as a generic model. For `h`: scenes in the library
   generated by a larger model from actual corpus passages, with `h` as a participant speaking in lines
   sampled from `h` itself and kept only when a judge with the passage in hand accepts them. The
   protective set is scenes where someone asks it to be an assistant and it declines in character.
- **Persona stability at inference.** "Measuring and Controlling Persona Drift" (arXiv 2402.10962;
   github.com/likenneth/persona_drift) gives a drift probe and split-softmax, a training-free control that
   re-weights attention toward the frame. Use the probe as an evaluation over long rooms; use the control
   only if drift is measured.
- **Aesthetic preference, one epoch.** Reactions and explicit chosen/rejected pairs from the room feed a
   small DPO on *voice*, capped at one epoch (TII's own warning), never on helpfulness.
- **Steering vectors as the knob.** Trained additive vectors for wet/dry and the other axes, with the
   controls FUTURETHOUGHT lists; the site's Wetness knob is their coefficient.

## What to avoid

Instruction mixtures (SmolTalk-style), long chain-of-thought traces, generic assistant SFT, and training on
the model's own unfiltered output. Each would erase the library faster than the corpus can restore it.

## Evaluation for the resident

RoomBench-H-lite before any of this trains: speak/silent accuracy on held-out When2Speak, addressee
accuracy, reply-target accuracy, and the evaluation pack's losses (plain, furniture-free, clean) so the
room mix is paid for in retention. The persona-drift probe over a 200-turn synthetic room. The haunting
scan on everything it says.
