format implemented by Hugging Face Transformers, and the chunked Mamba-2 SSD
factorization used by `jax-state-spaces/mamba2-jax`. Both source projects are
available under the Apache License 2.0.

- Falcon-H1 Transformers implementation:
  https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/blob/v4.57.1/src/transformers/models/falcon_h1/modeling_falcon_h1.py
- Mamba2-JAX implementation:
  https://github.com/jax-state-spaces/mamba2-jax
